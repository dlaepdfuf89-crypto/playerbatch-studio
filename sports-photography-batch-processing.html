BATCH ATHLETE GRAPHICS

Batch-process the roster, not one player at a time

Sports photography production becomes expensive when the same steps are repeated for every athlete: find the right portrait, enter player data, remove the background, apply team branding, export multiple sizes, rename files, and check for mistakes.

Batch-processing goal

Use the roster as the source of truth, process portraits consistently, surface exceptions, and generate standardized athlete graphics at team scale.

What belongs in the batch

  • Athlete-to-portrait matching
  • Name, number, position, and class data
  • Automatic local background removal
  • Team-color styling
  • Social and print export sizes
  • File naming and ZIP delivery
  • Manifest and failure reporting

What should not be hidden

Missing photos, duplicate athlete names, incomplete roster fields, and text that does not fit should be visible before the batch is considered finished. Good batch processing reduces repetitive work without hiding exceptions.

PlayerBatch Studio workflow

PlayerBatch Studio takes a roster CSV and portrait folder, auto-matches athletes, flags exceptions, performs local automatic background removal, renders four built-in sports visual systems, and exports 1080×1350 social PNGs plus 2400×3000 print PNGs.

Who this is for

The workflow is designed for youth sports photographers, school sports photographers, club photographers, and Media Day studios handling repeated player graphics across complete rosters.
