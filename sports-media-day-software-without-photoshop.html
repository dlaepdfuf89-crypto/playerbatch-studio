Why avoid a one-file-at-a-time loop?

The expensive part of a 20–100 athlete job is often repetition: matching the correct portrait, re-entering athlete data, applying the same design logic, creating two export sizes, and checking naming consistency. PlayerBatch is designed to batch those repeatable steps.

What still deserves human review?

Automation does not replace judgment. Portrait framing, unusual hair edges, text fit, team-color choices, and final commercial quality should still be reviewed before client delivery.
