What batch production should automate

Roster matching

Names, numbers, positions and portrait files should be paired once across the job rather than manually re-entered for each design.

Consistent rendering

One visual system should be applied across the entire team while athlete identity and team data change per export.

Exception review

Missing portraits, duplicate names and incomplete fields are more useful as a short review list than as silent failures inside a long manual workflow.

Delivery structure

Social and print outputs, plus manifest and failure reporting, make large jobs easier to inspect and deliver.
