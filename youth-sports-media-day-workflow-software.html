A practical post-shoot workflow

  1. Prepare a roster with athlete name, number, position, class, and portrait filename.
  2. Put clean PNG or JPG portraits into one folder.
  3. Match athletes to portraits across the roster.
  4. Review exceptions such as missing portraits, duplicate names, or long text.
  5. Run local automatic background removal and render the selected visual system.
  6. Export 1080×1350 social PNGs and 2400×3000 print PNGs.
  7. Deliver the batch with manifest and failure reporting.

Why roster-scale automation matters

Media Day jobs often involve 20, 30, 50, or more athletes who need the same visual treatment with different names, numbers, positions, and portraits. The biggest efficiency gain comes from automating the repeatable work while keeping exception review visible.

What should still be reviewed by a person?

Automating production does not remove the need for visual judgment. Portrait framing, unusual hair edges, text fit, team-color choices, and final output quality still deserve a human check before delivery.
